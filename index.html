Power Markets

Hourly demand, generation mix and grid conditions across eight North American markets, from official grid operator data.

How to read this

Conditions

Near peak means current demand is at least 95% of the highest hourly demand in the last 30 days. Above forecast means demand is running at least 4% over the operator's day-ahead forecast. Otherwise the market shows Normal.

These are simple, transparent signals, not a reserve-margin calculation.

Units

Hourly values are energy per hour (MWh), shown here as average power in GW. Clean share counts nuclear, wind, solar and hydro. Net imports are taken from the operator's reported interchange.

Sources

US markets: EIA Hourly Electric Grid Monitor (form EIA-930) via the EIA Open Data API. Recent hours are preliminary and can be revised.

Alberta: AESO public reports for pool price, demand and live supply. Fuel mix is sampled hourly.
